arrange battle mode — three layout directions

Lo-fi and clickable. Same fake song in all three, same audition model (click a candidate, it crosses over at the next bar line). Judge the structure, not the polish.

A · Stage rail

One decision at a time. Completed stages collapse to chips across the top; lineage sits in a sidebar.

Try: click through the five stages; the rail fills in behind you. Drag the mood pad on stage 03.

Strong: clearest first run, least to learn. Weak: tweaking an earlier choice means leaving where you are.

B · Workbench

The song is always on screen. An alternatives tray stays pointed at whatever you have selected — a stage, or a single voice.

Try: click a layer on the left (melody, brass…) and watch the tray retarget; toggle 🔒; pick Mood to swap the tray for the pad.

Strong: small tweaks are always one click away. Weak: nothing tells a newcomer where to start.

C · Lineage canvas

The history graph is the interface. Every choice is a node you can play, pin, or branch from; siblings are the alternatives.

Try: select a node and press ⟳ 4 more to spawn siblings, then → next stage to grow a child. Notice nothing is ever destroyed.

Strong: exploration is visible and comparison is spatial. Weak: gets crowded fast — needs collapsing and pruning.
